## Decided

- Continued using the click→screenshot→"back"→"Show all"→re-snapshot loop for every individual crypto payment tile rather than trusting the accessibility-tree text (which does list name+minimum for all 20 methods pre-expansion) — the skill's pitfalls table explicitly requires reading iframe content from the screenshot, not the DOM, and the crypto tiles here are true cross-origin iframes even though the fiat tiles on the same list are not.
- Treated `vip_casino_programs` and `vip_betting_programs` as satisfied by one shared screenshot with a cross-reference note, rather than marking one `not_found` — MafiaCasino's VIP ladder is explicitly unified across verticals on the page itself, not merely undocumented for one vertical.
